Dataset Summary

  • Dataset: Amazon Cell Phones Reviews (Kaggle).
  • CSV files: 20191226-items.csv and 20191226-reviews.csv
  • Classes: Positive Sentiment (4-5 stars) and Negative Sentiment (1-2 stars).
  • Records: 498 reviews mapped to their respective brands via ASIN joining.
  • Split: 75% training and 25% testing using stratification.

Model Configuration

  • Text preprocessing: English stop-word removal.
  • Feature extraction: TF-IDF with unigrams and bigrams.
  • Classifier: Multinomial Naïve Bayes.
  • Smoothing: alpha=0.10.
  • Evaluation: accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and Confusion Matrix.
