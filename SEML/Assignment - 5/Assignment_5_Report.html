Title

Sequence Diagrams for Open Ocean Cleanup

Objective

To design and implement detailed sequence diagrams mapping out the interactions between various components, actors, and databases for the Open Ocean Cleanup system.

Introduction

Sequence diagrams are interaction diagrams that detail how operations are carried out. They capture the behavior of a single scenario and show the sequence of messages passed between objects over time. For the Open Ocean Cleanup platform, we have designed four detailed sequence diagrams outlining core functionalities.

Key System Components Identified

  • Player: The end-user interacting with the game.
  • Game UI: The frontend interface displayed to the user.
  • Authentication System: Manages login and registration functionality.
  • Waste Sale System: Handles the logic of selecting and selling collected ocean waste.
  • Boat / Upgrades: Manages player vehicles, stats, and upgrade capabilities.
  • Coin Wallet: Tracks the player's currency.
  • Database: The persistent data storage system.
